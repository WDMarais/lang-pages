The Series

同一个灶，两种火

每一课都从一道真的做过的菜学词 — 不是背单词表，是照着菜谱下厨房。 Each lesson teaches its vocabulary through a dish actually cooked, from a real recipe — not a word list.

两道一起学才有意思：它们坐在同一条轴的两端。宫保鸡丁是辣、酱、繁； 麻油蛋炒饭是温、盐、简。做法的动词大多重合， 所以第二道菜，你已经会了一半。 Doing both is the point — they sit at opposite ends of one axis. Kung Pao is heat, sauce, complexity; the fried rice is warmth, salt, restraint. The wok verbs mostly overlap, so the second dish is already half-learned.

Carry-Over

重合的词

这五个词两道菜都用得上 — 学完第一道，它们在第二道里就是免费的。这就是把两道菜编成一个系列的意思。 These five words appear in both dishes — learn them once in the first lesson and they come free in the second. That is what makes the two a series rather than two separate decks.
