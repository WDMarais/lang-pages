# Projection Views — Design Notes / Roadmap

The content graph (`data/nodes.json` + `edges.json` + `bindings.json`, generated by
`data/build-graph.py` from the hand-authored card sources) is **one substrate
consumed by several projection views**. Architecture: one tagged substrate on a
shared, language-neutral **referent spine**; each view is a predicate over the
substrate. See `docs/content-graph-schema.md` for the graph itself.

Two orthogonal families of view:

- **Audience skins** (glyph-rooted) — Han-comparative / CN-centered / JP-centered.
  Differ by which bindings, words, and notes are in scope. Converge at the
  character/concept tier (one shared node + CN/JP bindings), diverge at the word
  tier (separate audience-tagged word nodes, rejoining at the shared referent).
- **Structural consumers** (differ by root entity and disclosure level):
  1. **Author/structure** — the graph with its seams showing (frontier stubs, node
     ids, edge kinds, coverage). For growing and verifying the DAG.
  2. **Encyclopedia/browse** — referent-rooted, disclosure turned down.
  3. **SRS/atomized** — one edge at a time (prompt A → answer B → grade). The
     graph's *edges are the SRS pairs*; SRS is the graph shattered per-edge.

## JP page — future work

- **Referent-illustration ladder** (the `.jp-referent` slot):
  1. Ten-frame quantity dots for number referents — **done**.
  2. **正-tally delight-version** — illustrate the count the East Asian way, in 正
     units of 5. Reuses the stroke-render tech (HanziWriter) we already have; tiles
     every 5. The on-theme option, to pair with cultural notes.
  3. **Per-referent icons** for non-number referents (入 / 人 / 山 …). The reserved
     empty slot (`.jp-illus-todo`) already exists for this.
- **Cultural notes** — new first-class per-(node, language) data: 八重垣 / 八百万 /
  末広がり on 八, 七夕 vs 七五三, etc. Not in the schema yet; deliberate add. Attaches
  as enriched bindings.
- **Numbers > 10** — the ten-frame caps at ten. If larger number referents appear,
  stack frames (base-10) rather than growing one frame.

## Content selection

**Concrete-first**: the practical JP/CN pages prefer referents that anchor to
something visualizable/mnemonic (seven, person, enter, mountain). Abstract
referents (economic contraction, nominalizers) resist the glyph-mnemonic +
illustration approach — keep them in the substrate but defer them to the
less-visual consumers (encyclopedia, SRS), and off the front of the practical
pages. The ten-frame / 正-tally illustration work is this principle made visual.
