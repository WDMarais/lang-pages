Counting one to ten数一到十

一 到 十

A number's referent is a quantity. Shown two ways — pips clustered in fives, and the 正 tally, the East-Asian 5-stroke counting mark (each 正 = 5).

Powers of ten — the myriad system位 · 万进制

位

Place-values 十百千 cycle inside each block; blocks are named every four digits — 万 (10⁴), 億 (10⁸) — not every three. So big numbers group as 1,0000 and 1,0000,0000, not 10,000 / 100,000,000.
